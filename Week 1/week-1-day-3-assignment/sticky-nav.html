Destinations

Kenya offers an incredible range of destinations, from the white sand beaches of Diani to the snow-capped peaks of Mount Kenya. The Great Rift Valley cuts through the country, offering dramatic landscapes and volcanic lakes. Coastal towns like Mombasa and Lamu blend Swahili architecture with centuries of trade history, while Nairobi serves as a bustling gateway to it all.

Wildlife

Home to the Big Five and countless other species, Kenya's national parks and reserves are a haven for wildlife enthusiasts. The annual wildebeest migration across the Maasai Mara is one of nature's most spectacular events. Lake Nakuru is famous for its flocks of flamingos, while Amboseli offers unforgettable views of elephants roaming beneath Mount Kilimanjaro.

Culture

Kenya is home to over 40 ethnic communities, each with its own traditions, languages, and customs. From Maasai beadwork and dance to Swahili coastal cuisine, the country's cultural diversity is one of its greatest treasures. Nairobi's art galleries, music scene, and food markets reflect a modern identity shaped by this rich heritage.
